Title: ATS Resume Formatting Guidelines
Category: ats_guidelines

Use standard section headings such as Experience,
Education, Skills, and Projects.

Prefer a simple, single-column layout when
compatibility with automated resume parsers matters.

Avoid placing essential contact information
inside headers, footers, or images.

Use relevant keywords naturally when they
accurately describe the applicant's experience.

Follow the file format requested by the employer.

ATS systems differ, so no formatting approach
guarantees successful parsing or selection.
