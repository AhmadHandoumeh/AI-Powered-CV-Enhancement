Title: Tailoring Resumes to Job Descriptions
Category: job_applications

Identify the most relevant skills, qualifications,
and responsibilities in the job description.

Compare these requirements against evidence
in the applicant's resume.

Prioritize relevant experience and projects.

Use terminology from the job description when
it accurately reflects the applicant's background.

Identify missing qualifications without claiming
the applicant possesses them.

Recommend improvements based on demonstrated
experience rather than keyword stuffing.
